# Rights and Preservation

## Historical Software

The surviving Tycoon Tex source credits the concept to Matthew Clarke, Anthony Clarke, and Ian Stewart, and programming to Micro Projects for Gremlin Graphics Ltd., with 1985 notices. These notices remain in the preserved source. The supplied game binary and historical source retain their existing rights status.

---

## Printed Material and Artwork

The supplied images, cassette packaging, scrapbook pages, articles, advertisements, logos, and artwork retain the rights of their respective holders. The [artifact catalogue](docs/ARTIFACTS.md) identifies the supplied material.

---

## Repository Status

This repository prepares the historical material for study, reassembly, and preservation. No new open-source or artwork licence has been assigned in this preparation. Public availability, if the owner chooses to publish the repository, should not be read as a grant of rights beyond an expressly supplied licence.

The build scripts adapt the approach used in Anthony Clarke's [Dorks Dilemma C16 repository](https://github.com/anthonyjclarke/Dorks_Dilemma_C16). Recovery background and tool acknowledgements are maintained there. The original input files are not rewritten by the build.
