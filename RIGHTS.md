# Rights and Preservation

## Historical Software

The surviving Tycoon Tex source credits the concept to Matthew Clarke, Anthony Clarke, and Ian Stewart, and the programming to Micro Projects for Gremlin Graphics Ltd., with 1985 notices. Those notices are still in the preserved source. The game binary and the historical source keep whatever rights status they already had.

---

## Printed Material and Artwork

The images, cassette packaging, scrapbook pages, articles, advertisements, logos and artwork here stay with their respective rights holders. The [artifact catalogue](docs/ARTIFACTS.md) lists what I've included.

---

## Repository Status

I've set this repository up for studying, reassembling and preserving the historical material. I haven't assigned any new open-source or artwork licence. Making the repository public doesn't grant any rights beyond a licence that's stated explicitly.

The build scripts adapt the approach from my [Dorks Dilemma C16 repository](https://github.com/anthonyjclarke/Dorks_Dilemma_C16), where the recovery background and tool acknowledgements are kept. The build never rewrites the original input files.
